Buenos Aires with Kids: Steak, Tango, and a City That Lives at Night

BA runs on a different schedule — dinner at 9pm, parks alive at midnight. This is actually perfect for families who nap, and once you sync with its rhythm, the city is extraordinarily generous with children.

  • Palermo parks complex: zoo, Japanese garden, paddleboat lake, planetarium — full day sorted
  • MALBA (Latin American art museum) has a genuine kids section and is world-class
  • San Telmo Sunday market: antiques, street food, tango dancers performing in the street
  • Tigre Delta day trip — boat through the Paraná river delta, completely otherworldly
  • Steak at a parrilla: this is what you came for. Order the bife de chorizo.
