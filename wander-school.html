About the School

A Private School
Built for Wanderers

Wander School is a registered private home study program under Georgia law, offering enrichment education for young learners ready to move through the world with confidence and grace. Our curriculum spans life skills, global etiquette, cultural literacy, and the practical knowledge children need to thrive anywhere on earth.


Classes are offered through Outschool, making expert instruction accessible to families everywhere — whether you're raising your child at home, supplementing a traditional education, or preparing your young traveler for the world ahead.

Curriculum

What Your Child Will Learn

🌍

World Exploration

Geography, maps, and movement. Students learn how to read the world around them — from regions and climates to navigating new places with curiosity and confidence.

🤝

Cultural Etiquette

The customs and courtesies that open doors everywhere. Greetings, dining, dress, and the art of fitting in without losing yourself — across every culture.

🧳

Life Skills

Real-world independence for young learners. Budgeting, packing, communication, and the practical tools children need to move through any situation with ease.

🌐

Global Citizenship

What it means to belong to the world. History, current events, and the empathy needed to be a thoughtful, responsible citizen of our global community.
